Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

This website

monoonworks.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and its fonts are served from this domain. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email and the beta list

If you write to contact@monoonworks.com, including to join the beta list, we keep your message and email address to reply and to let you know when a beta place opens. We do not sell or share them. Mail is hosted by Zoho Mail. Ask us at any time and we will delete what you sent.

Our apps

Academia Flow, Academia Archive, Simple PDF, Simple Note and Simple Search keep your research data on your own device. Monoon Works does not run servers that receive your documents, notes or library. Optional connections, such as Zotero, connect directly from your device to that service when you set them up.

Claude in Simple PDF

Simple PDF can explain passages and summarise pages with either a model that runs on your Mac or Claude through the Anthropic API. You choose the engine in the paper notes panel.

  • With the on-device engine, nothing is sent off your Mac.
  • With the Claude engine, Simple PDF sends the text of the pages in the range you choose, your question, a fixed instruction template and any figure region you select directly from your device to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com), using the API key you provide.
  • The PDF file itself, your highlights and your memos are not sent.
  • Monoon Works does not receive or store these requests. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • Your API key is kept in the macOS Keychain on that device. Generated summaries are cached on your Mac so the same request is not sent twice.

Other optional services in Simple PDF

If you connect DeepL for translation, the text you choose to translate is sent to DeepL. To fill in citation details, Simple PDF can look up a document’s DOI with Crossref and doi.org; only the DOI is sent.

Simple PDF의 Claude

Simple PDF는 Mac에서 실행되는 모델 또는 Anthropic API를 통한 Claude로 구절을 설명하고 쪽을 정리합니다. 엔진은 논문 정리 패널에서 고릅니다.

  • 이 Mac 엔진은 아무것도 밖으로 보내지 않습니다.
  • Claude 엔진은 선택한 범위의 쪽 텍스트, 질문, 고정된 지시 템플릿, 직접 고른 그림 영역을 사용자가 입력한 API 키로 사용자의 기기에서 Anthropic API(api.anthropic.com)로 직접 보냅니다.
  • PDF 파일 자체, 하이라이트, 메모는 보내지 않습니다.
  • Monoon Works는 이 요청을 받거나 저장하지 않습니다. Anthropic이 자사 상업 약관과 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 처리합니다.
  • API 키는 그 기기의 macOS 키체인에 저장합니다. 같은 요청을 다시 보내지 않도록 생성된 정리는 Mac에 캐시합니다.

Simple PDF의 다른 선택 기능

DeepL 번역을 연결하면 번역하려고 고른 텍스트가 DeepL로 전송됩니다. 인용 정보를 채우기 위해 문서의 DOI로 Crossref와 doi.org를 조회할 수 있으며, DOI만 전송합니다.
