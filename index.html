SaegimReading, notes, writing and planning in one workspace

The many hours of a research project, in one place.

Saegim holds the schedule, the reading, the notes and the writing of a long project together, so work that moves at different speeds can be seen in one workspace.

Quotations keep their page numbers. Notes can be checked against the source they came from. A chapter draft stays next to the evidence that made it possible.

Connected at the source, ready to return to.

One source, across Galpi and Saegim.

  1. Keep the record in Galpi

    The bibliography, attachments, transcription and notes stay on the record: not only what the source is, but where it came from and how it was read.

  2. Bring it into Saegim

    Search Galpi from the reading list and link the record. The bibliography comes along, with a link back to the original record.

  3. Read, note, and go back

    Write the reading note and page-numbered quotations in Saegim. “Archive linked” shows the connection; “Open Archive” returns to the record in Galpi.

The link carries the bibliography and the way back to the record. Original files, OCR, transcriptions and Galpi notes are not copied or synced into Saegim.

Simple.
Straight to the task.

Reading, writing and finding: three everyday motions, three small macOS tools that share one document shape and one way of naming things.

Simple PDFA macOS reader with the endnotes beside the text

Read the text without losing the endnotes.

The body stays on its page while the notes open in a second pane that can show a different page.

Move between chapters through the outline, keep your reading position, and leave quotations and notes on the document.

Ask about a passage, and keep the page.

Select a passage and ask. The answer comes back in plain language, and every sentence carries the page it rests on, so it can be checked against the original.

Summaries work the same way, up to forty pages at a time, section by section. Only the page text in range, your question and any figure region you select are sent; the PDF file, highlights and memos are not. It can also run entirely on a model on your Mac.

Simple NoteA local note editor, rich text and Markdown

A sentence, straight into your file.

Start with a line. Add headings, a checklist, a table or a quotation when the thought needs structure.

Switch between rich text and Markdown, tag and search notes, open local Markdown files, and export a note to keep working elsewhere.

Simple SearchMenu-bar file search by name or Korean initials

The name you remember, the file you need.

Type part of a name, or just the Korean initial consonants, and the file is there. Try it on the right.

The index uses file names and metadata, not document contents. Narrow by type, preview with Quick Look, open or reveal in Finder, all from the keyboard.

Monoon Works began with the everyday work of graduate research: following a bibliography, returning to a passage, and turning scattered notes into an argument.

Zotero, Tropy and EndNote each have a place in that work. These tools grew from a question that remained between them: how can sources, notes, writing and planning stay connected as a project grows?

The tools are now in a small private pilot, and research practice keeps deciding what gets built next. A public beta is planned for the first half of 2027.

A few practical questions.

When can I use it?

The tools are in a small private pilot now, and a public beta is planned for the first half of 2027. Join the beta list to hear first, or write to ask about the pilot.

What does Simple PDF send when I ask about a passage?

Only the page text in range, your question and any figure region you select. The PDF file, highlights and memos are never sent. You can also choose a model that runs on your Mac, so nothing leaves it. Which services are involved is in the privacy notice.

Where is my research stored?

Saegim stores its research workspace locally on your device. Galpi also keeps its source files and database locally. Optional connections, such as Zotero imports, are configured separately.

Can I keep using Zotero and Word?

Yes. Galpi can import Zotero records, and the companion Word add-in supports literature search, citations, footnotes and bibliographies.

How do Saegim and Galpi work together?

Galpi keeps source records, original files and their context. Saegim connects reading, notes, chapter writing and planning. Selected Galpi records can be linked to Saegim.
