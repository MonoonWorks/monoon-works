Simple PDF · Claude engineNew in pilot

Ask the paper.
Keep the page.

Select a passage in Simple PDF and ask about it. Claude explains it in plain language and attaches the page each sentence rests on, so every answer can be checked against the original.

Summaries work the same way: up to forty pages at a time, section by section, with page references and a short map of how the argument moves.

  • Grounded in the textAnswers use only the pages you give them. Numbers stay as printed, and what the excerpt cannot support is said plainly.
  • You choose what is sentOnly the page text in range, your question and any figure region you select go to the Claude API — never the PDF file, your highlights or your memos.
  • Local when you want itSwitch the same panel to a model that runs on your Mac, and nothing leaves it.
  • Kept in your KeychainThe API key is stored in the macOS Keychain on that device and checked before it is saved.

HVNGraduate researcher · founder & developer · Seoul

Monoon Works began with the everyday work of graduate research: following a bibliography, returning to a passage, and turning scattered notes into an argument.

Zotero, Tropy, and EndNote each have a place in that work. These tools grew from a question that remained between them: how can sources, notes, writing, and planning stay connected as a project grows?

The tools are now in a small private pilot, and research practice keeps deciding what gets built next. A public beta is planned for the first half of 2027.

A few practical questions.

When can I use it?

The tools are in a small private pilot now, and a public beta is planned for the first half of 2027. Join the beta list to hear first, or write to ask about the pilot.

Which AI does Simple PDF use, and what is sent?

You choose for each panel: Claude Opus 5.5 through the Claude API, or a model that runs on your Mac. With Claude, only the page text in range, your question and any figure region you select are sent to Anthropic’s API. The PDF file, highlights and memos are not. See the privacy notice.

Where is my research stored?

Flow stores its research workspace locally on your device. Archive also keeps its source files and database locally. Optional connections, such as Zotero imports, are configured separately.

Can I keep using Zotero and Word?

Yes. Archive can import Zotero records, and the companion Word add-in supports literature search, citations, footnotes, and bibliographies.

How do Flow and Archive work together?

Archive keeps source records, original files, and their context. Flow connects reading, notes, chapter writing, and planning. Selected Archive records can be linked to Flow.

Can I change the appearance?

Flow offers multiple light, dark, and special themes, with Light, Dark, and Auto modes. This page shows the app in Academic Neutral.
